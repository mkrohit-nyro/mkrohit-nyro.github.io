Help & Shortcuts

📲 Nyro on your phone

This is the Nyro web app. Sign in with the same email as on your laptop: your chats, memory, notes, to-dos, Pro plan and Inbox are the same everywhere.
iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu ⋮ → Install app.

🗣️ Talk to Nyro

Tap 🎤 to open voice mode and speak. Nyro answers aloud and listens again. Change the language in ⚙ Settings → Speech language. Tap ✕ to close.

💬 Chat and Task

Chat: talk, answers, notes, memory.
Task: also does things: reminders, to-dos, photos, search, Facebook and Instagram posts (Pro).

🖼️ Zylos-Gen 1.1

Pick Zylos-Gen 1.1 in the model menu and describe a photo. Choose the shape and quality above the message box. Add a photo with + to edit it. Tap a photo to open or save it.

🌐 Search, links & files

Ask about today's news, prices or scores, or paste any link and ask what it says. Add a photo, PDF or text file with + and ask about it.

⏰ Reminders

“Remind me at 5 pm to take my medicine.” On the phone, reminders ring while Nyro is open. Turn on notifications in ⚙ Settings. Reminders made here stay on this phone.

💻 Only in the Windows app

Opening apps, music and volume, file search, screenshots, “what's on my screen”, Gmail and the F8 shortcut.

🔐 Permission

Nyro asks before posting or deleting. Everything else runs directly.
